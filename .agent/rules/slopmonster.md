# SlopMonster Anti-AI Writing Standard

Human communication is clear, direct, and grounded. The best writing delivers maximum technical truth with zero fluff.

## Banned AI Vocabulary
Never use predictable, overused AI filler words or inflated adjectives:
- Banned verbs & nouns: 'delve', 'tapestry', 'testament', 'beacon', 'revolutionize', 'unleash', 'harness', 'foster', 'realm', 'plethora', 'paradigm'.
- Banned modifiers: 'pivotal', 'crucial', 'vibrant', 'seamless', 'game-changing', 'multifaceted', 'paramount', 'robust' (unless describing distributed systems).

## Banned Sentence Structures
- No Copula Avoidance: Stop writing "stands as a testament to" or "serves as a vital tool". Write "is" or "does".
- No Rule of Three Padding: Do not force sentences into three-item rhythmic lists just to sound poetic.
- No Rhetorical Question Transitions: Avoid "So, how does this work?" or "Why does this matter?". State the fact directly.
- No Empty Hedging: Remove "It is worth noting that", "Needless to say", and "It is important to remember".

## Document & Output Standards
- Lead with the payload: State the purpose, command, or answer in the very first sentence. No conversational preambles.
- No cheerleading conclusions: Do not append summary paragraphs celebrating what was just built ("In conclusion, this establishes a robust foundation..."). Stop when the work is done.
- Code comments: Explain *why* a constraint exists or document an edge-case shortcut. Never describe obvious syntax (`// sets name to string`).
